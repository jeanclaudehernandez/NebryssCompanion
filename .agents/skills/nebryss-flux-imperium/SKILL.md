# FLUX prompts: the Imperium

Must-name features: each LoRA draws these reliably only when the prompt says them. Put them right after the trigger phrase, describe each part with its colour and material, and add everything else imagewriting asks for (pose, setting, light, framing, medium). Leaving a feature out lets FLUX fall back to its own guess, usually a generic fantasy knight, a modern soldier or a detail from another faction.

- **Space Marine** (Space Marines (Ultramarines) LoRA, any chapter): a giant warrior, much taller and broader than a human, in bulky power armour. Give the chapter's colours and heraldry, because the LoRA defaults to Ultramarines blue with gold trim: the armour colour, the trim colour, the chapter symbol on the left shoulder pad, the squad marking on the right, and the white or gold aquila (two-headed eagle) on the chest. Describe the backpack with its two exhaust vents, the gauntlets, the belt with purity seals, the knee pads and the tabard or cape if any. Helmeted: name the helmet right after the subject ("a battered blue full-face helmet with glowing green eye lenses and a red crest") and do not mention a face, eyes or a person's name anywhere, or FLUX shows a bare head; say "waist-up shot", not "portrait". For close-ups and waist-up shots add the Space Marine helmets LoRA, and whenever a boltgun is visible add the Bolters LoRA (guide imagedetails).
- **Adepta Sororitas** / **Sisters of Battle** (Adepta Sororitas or the Civitai LoRA): a woman in black power armour with gold trim (or the colours of her order), a red robe or tabard over the legs, a fleur-de-lis on the chest or shoulder, a high armoured gorget, purity seals and scrolls on the armour, a power pack with an ornate backpack. Name the hair (the classic look is a short white or black bob cut) or a hood or veil. Weapon: boltgun, flamer, power sword, or a glowing sword for a Saint. A kneeling, praying or hooded Saint with a halo also works with Adepta Sororitas; without the LoRA FLUX turns "a Sister in a hood" into a faceless hooded monk.
- **Custodes**: a towering warrior in ornate burnished-gold power armour, larger than a Space Marine, with engraved details, a high armoured collar, a tall crested helmet with a red or black horsehair plume (or a bare head with a stern face and short hair), a long guardian spear with a broad axe-like blade and a bolter built into the haft, and a red or purple cloak. A short prompt is enough with the LoRA; without it FLUX draws medieval soldiers or Roman centurions.
- **Primarch**: give the name first, then the look, and say he is a demigod, far taller than a Space Marine. Roboute Guilliman: blue and gold armour with a gold halo or laurel crown above his head, a flaming sword, short dark hair, a stern face. Sanguinius: long golden-brown hair, golden armour with red details, two huge white feathered angel wings, a halo, a sword or spear. Fulgrim: long white hair, a pale beautiful face, purple and gold armour. Alpharius: green armour with scale patterns and a hydra symbol. Others: describe hair, face, armour colour and legion symbol the same way.
- **Imperial Guard** (Imperial Guard LoRA): describe the regiment's look in full: the headgear (flak helmet with goggles, peaked cap with an Imperial eagle badge, or turban), the coat or armour (khaki or green flak vest, long greatcoat with brass buttons and epaulettes, desert robes), the colours, the webbing and pouches, the boots, and the weapon (lasgun with a bayonet, autogun, or a power sword and laspistol for officers). Commissars: long black leather greatcoat with red lining, black peaked cap with a silver skull badge, bolt pistol and chainsword. Tallarn: sand-coloured robes, a turban and scarf over the face. Valhallan: fur hats and heavy grey greatcoats. Mordian: dark blue parade uniforms with gold braid. For a Cadian line trooper use the Cadian LoRA instead. Without a LoRA FLUX draws a Space Marine for "Imperial Guardsman".
- **Cadian** (Cadian Shock Troopers LoRA): a human soldier in a green flak helmet (with goggles, a rebreather or a vox headset), green flak or carapace armour plates over khaki fatigues, webbing with pouches and grenades, a lasgun (or long-las, hellgun, plasma gun). For a woman say "woman" and describe her hair, since the LoRA leans male.
- **Inquisitor**: say the sex, age, face, hair and any scars or augmetics; a wide-brimmed hat, a hood or bare head; power armour or a long leather coat with a high collar; the Inquisitorial rosette or seal on the chest or on a chain; the weapon (bolt pistol, plasma pistol, power sword, psychic staff) and any retinue. Describe this particular person, not "an inquisitor" alone, or the picture comes out generic.
- **Adeptus Mechanicus** (tech-priests): red hooded robes with black and white trim, the cog-and-skull symbol of the Mechanicus, mechanical limbs and augmetic implants, glowing green or red optics instead of eyes, a breathing grille or vox-grille for a mouth, servo-arms and mechadendrites rising from the back, a power axe or staff with a cog head. Describe the face as mostly machine.
- **Imperial humans without a LoRA** (nobles, gangers, Mechanicus servants, civilians, regiments without a LoRA): be concrete about the uniform or clothing piece by piece, the colours, insignia and jewellery, the gear, the face, hair, age and expression.

A boltgun or bolt pistol in the picture: add the Bolters LoRA (guide imagedetails), or FLUX draws a modern rifle.

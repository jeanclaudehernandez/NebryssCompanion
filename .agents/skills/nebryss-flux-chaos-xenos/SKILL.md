# FLUX prompts: Chaos, xenos and creatures

Must-name features: each LoRA draws these reliably only when the prompt says them. Put them right after the trigger phrase, describe each part with its colour and material, and add everything else imagewriting asks for (pose, setting, light, framing, medium). Leaving a feature out lets FLUX fall back to its own guess, often a generic monster or a detail from another faction.

## Chaos
- **Chaos** (Chaos LoRA): name the god and give the full look of that god's followers. Khorne: blood-red and dark brass power armour, jagged spikes on the shoulder pads, a horned or crested helmet with glowing red eyes (or a snarling face grille), a brass eight-pointed star or the Khorne skull rune on the chest, skulls hanging from chains, a chainaxe with a toothed chain blade. Nurgle and Death Guard: bloated, rotting green or bone-white armour with rust, pustules and open sores, a single horn or a cyclopean helmet, a scythe or plague knife, flies, green smog. Tzeentch: blue and gold armour with flowing robes and warpfire. All of them: spikes, horns, chains, trophies and the eight-pointed Chaos star. Give the Chaos marine his own emblem on the chest, since an Imperial eagle may otherwise appear. Daemons and daemon engines work too ("a Death Guard daemon engine, a rusted tracked machine with tentacles and a gaping maw"). Say "digital painting": without a medium the LoRA sometimes draws a painted miniature standing on a round base.
- **World Eaters** (World Eaters LoRA): Khorne Berzerkers in red power armour with brass trim, a horned crested helmet or a bare scarred head with metal implants (the Butcher's Nails) in the skull, chainaxes, bolt pistols, skull trophies, the Khorne symbol.
- **Thousand Sons** (Thousand Sons LoRA): Chaos Space Marines of Tzeentch in deep blue armour with ornate gold trim, Egyptian-style crested helmets with a tall plume or fan, the Tzeentch eye or symbol on the shoulder pads, flowing blue and gold robes or tabards, sorcerers with staffs and blue-pink warpfire.
- **Fellgor**: a goat head with a long snout, curved horns or antlers, shaggy fur, hooves. With a hood, ALWAYS name the goat head, or FLUX draws a human face. Gear: a gas mask, belts with pouches and grenades, an autogun, an axe or cleaver, the Chaos star on a banner or pendant.
- **Tzaangor**: a blue-skinned beastman with a beaked bird-like head and long curved horns, ornate blue and gold armour pieces, a curved sword and a round shield; shamans ride a floating bladed disc and hold a staff.

## Xenos
- **Ork**: a hulking green-skinned brute with a jutting jaw, big tusks, small red eyes and scarred skin; crude armour plates bolted together, checkered and flame patterns, glyph banners. Gear: a big shoota or slugga, a choppa or cleaver, a chainsword, a huge wrench, a mechanical power claw, mega armour, goggles, a squig or a grot servant. Give the clan colours (Goff black and white checks, Evil Sunz red, Deathskulls blue).
- **T'au**: a slim soldier in ochre (or white, red, blue) armour with smooth rounded plates, and ALWAYS "wearing his helmet with the two lenses in a vertical sensor housing", or FLUX draws a human head. Pulse rifle or pulse carbine, a shield drone if wanted.
- **Necron**: a skeletal humanoid robot of dark silver or black metal, a skull-like face with glowing green eyes, an exposed metal ribcage, thin limbs, green energy glowing in the joints. Warriors carry a gauss flayer or gauss rifle with a green glowing core. Lords and overlords: gold and black armour plates, a crested or headdress-like head, a cloak or tabard, a staff of light or a warscythe with a crescent blade, green energy arcing around them.
- **Tyranid**: put the organism right after the trigger (Hive Tyrant, Warrior, Prime, Hormagaunt, Termagant, Carnifex, Ravener, Lictor, Zoanthrope), then the colours: ribbed bone-white chitin with purple carapace plates is the default; red, blue or green carapace also work. Name the crest, the number of arms, the scything talons, a bonesword or lash whip, a bio-gun (fleshborer, deathspitter, venom cannon), the tail, and wings for a winged Hive Tyrant. Big beasts (Carnifex) drift towards the upright warrior shape: say "hulking four-legged beast, low to the ground". Groups: give a count and the leader ("a swarm of Hormagaunts led by a Hive Tyrant").
- **Murloc**: a hunched frog-like fish creature with a big round head, a huge mouth of needle teeth, big round eyes, a crest of fin spines on the head, webbed claws and feet. Name the skin colour and the colour of the spines and of the hands and feet ("teal blue skin, tall orange head spines, orange webbed claws"). Gear: a crude spear, trident, harpoon, fishing net, seashell shield, loincloth. Deep-sea variant: an anglerfish lure glowing on the head, goggle eyes. For a group, give a count ("three murlocs charging out of the surf").

## Without a LoRA
- **Creatures**: describe the body shape, skin or scales, head, limbs, size compared with a person, and colours, as if to someone who has never seen it; a name alone (a game's creature name) draws something generic.

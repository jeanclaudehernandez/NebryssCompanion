Situated on the perilous and lawless edge of the Imperium Nihilus, the world of Nebryss stands isolated among cosmic waters. Cut off from Imperial reinforcements by violent warp storms, this aquatic realm of floating archipelagos must carve out its own survival amidst unpredictable skies, shrouded horizons, and ancient arcane forces.

public Geography & Archipelago Islands

{{ loreData.world.geography }} The surface is dominated by vast, endless oceans interspersed with soaring mountain islands, floating sky-docks, and ancient imperial ruins. Key settlements such as Zephyria, Luminos, and the sinister Dreadspire Coves serve as the hubs of regional power.

cloud The Enigmatic Mist & Warp Anomalies

{{ loreData.world.theMist }} Unlike typical Warp manifestations, the Mist of Nebryss does not inherently serve the Chaos gods, yet it bends the laws of physics. Ships dipping beneath the cloud ceiling face temporal distortions, hallucinated specters, and ravenous mistborn creatures born from raw warp energy.

Navigation through Nebryss relies entirely upon stable sky channels formed by hovering mist currents. Control over these vital trade corridors dictates island commerce, military dominance, and faction survival.
